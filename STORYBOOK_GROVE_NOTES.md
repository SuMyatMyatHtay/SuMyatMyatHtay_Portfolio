# Storybook Grove update

The home-page storytelling section is now an original interactive storybook tree inspired by the supplied cozy game-UI reference.

## Three branches
- Achievements: Academic Prizes + Sports Prizes
- Campus & Community: Student Life + Community Service
- Little Pieces of Me: Hobbies + Where I'm From (Myanmar)

This keeps all six of your story topics while matching the three-branch visual structure from the reference.

## Images to add
Place your real photos at:
- public/story/academic.jpg
- public/story/sports.jpg
- public/story/student-life.jpg
- public/story/community.jpg
- public/story/hobbies.jpg
- public/story/myanmar.jpg

If an image is missing, the ornament and modal show a built-in placeholder instead of breaking.

## Interactions
- Hover/focus/tap a category badge to light its branch and twigs.
- Each ornament has a subtle idle sway.
- Click/tap an ornament to open the storybook modal.
- Modal closes with the X, outside click, or Escape.
- prefers-reduced-motion disables the decorative animation.

## Themes
The layout is shared across themes, but the environment changes:
- Light: pink/cream princess storybook grove.
- Dark: navy/dusk magical forest with warmer glowing accents.
